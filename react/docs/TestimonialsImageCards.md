Portrait cards with customer quotes under a heading with slider arrows.

Testimonials Image Cards builds trust by putting real customers front and centre: each card pairs a portrait with a short quote and the author's name and role, while the label and heading frame the social proof. Use it on landing and product pages close to a call to action, where credibility helps people decide.

The header is a Container 50% + 50% — a Text group (label + heading) on the left and previous / next arrows (Button) on the right — over a Container of four 25% cards. Each card is a dark portrait with a bottom scrim so the quote stays legible. On mobile the cards become a horizontal carousel with the next card peeking in.

## Layout
- Desktop (> 1024px): The label and heading sit left with the prev/next arrows bottom-right; four portrait cards fill the 25% columns below, each with its quote and author over the photo.
- Mobile (≤ 1024px): The heading wraps on the left with the arrows beside it; the cards turn into a horizontal carousel, with the next card peeking in from the right.

## When to use
- Showing short quotes from customers, partners or users to build credibility.
- Putting a face to each endorsement so it feels personal and real.
- Supporting a sales or sign-up page right before or after a call to action.

## When not to use
- Long case studies or detailed reviews — use an article or a case-study section.
- Ratings, scores or aggregated reviews — use a stats or rating pattern.
- Quotes without a photo of the person — use a text-only quote or a simple card grid.
